Stores → Xero tracking

The tracking option is the Xero Store value put on every expense line (e.g. “Wellstone Park - EC”). The cash code is the store's Cash In/Out (POS) account — it's the account used by the cash-sales journal. The cash journal label is the short store name used on the Shopify comparison and its downloaded workbook (e.g. “Parkview Park” where the app says “Parkview Mall”). It does not appear in the journal CSV.

Cash-sales journal

The monthly cash-split journal puts each store's captured cash sales against its cash code above. Its final control-account row carries the balancing credit, so the journal totals zero and Xero accepts the import. Set that control account and the journal's narration here.
