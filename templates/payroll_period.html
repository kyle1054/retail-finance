Export deductions for a period

Choose a start and end month. The export lists every retail deduction (uniform, lay-by and undercharge) falling in each month of the range, one month-by-month block per month, with a monthly subtotal and an overall grand total. Ranges can cross a year.

What you get
  1. One block per month in the range
  2. Each block lists staff with deductions that month, grouped by store
  3. A subtotal per month and a grand total at the end
Only staff with a deduction in a given month appear in that month's block. Maximum range is 24 months.
