What makes a transaction ready for Xero?

A ready transaction has a receipt or is personal, has a reason and location, was submitted to finance, and has no receipt match awaiting a decision. An open VAT tax-invoice request also blocks readiness. Account coding is shown as a suggestion and does not block readiness.
